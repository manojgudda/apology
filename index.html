what they see / what i see

They see the confidence, the walls you've made, but I see the girl who learned too young to be afraid — shaped by the nights your home came undone, in a house that taught you love isn't promised to anyone.

eldest daughter, second mother

Eldest daughter, second mother, first to bleed, you learned to carry weight before you learned to read the signs of who would stay and who would leave — so now you brace for loss before you let yourself believe.

guarded, not gone

You loved before, and it left you guarded, not gone. Somewhere along the way, trust became hard to lean on. And I won't pretend I'm innocent in this — I'm folded into the story too, a name on the list of people who took something soft and gave back hurt.

i won't ask you to forget

That hurt was real. I won't ask you to forget it, or pretend it didn't happen just because I regret it.

but darling

But darling, I'm not asking you to forget the wound. I'm asking for a chance to be the proof that not every hand that reaches in will take — some stay just to hold what's left, and help you remake the parts of you that learned to brace for the fall.

slowly, in time

I don't need you to trust me all at once, not at all. Just let me show you slow, let me show you in time — that I see the whole of you, not just one line: the survivor, the protector, the soft girl who never got to just be a daughter, just be loved without conditions, without having to prove that she's worth staying for.

not a measurement, a fact

I'm not asking you to measure me by what's behind. I'm here for the woman who's still standing, still whole, despite every hand that tried to take pieces of her soul. You are.(worth staying for, worth loving — not just to put a worth on you)

i'm not going anywhere

Take your time. Doubt me if you need to. I'll still be here — not to chase, but to prove that some kinds of love don't run when it's hard. You can keep your walls. I'll wait outside the part if that still feels too risky to open today. Just know I'm not going anywhere. I'm here to stay.

one more thing, before you go

You don't have to open this. It'll wait as long as you need it to — outside the part that still feels too risky today.

open when you're ready

I'm sorry. Not the kind of sorry that wants something back — just the kind that means it, and waits.
